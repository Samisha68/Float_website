## Two concepts that won't change

The technical details may move. These two ideas are the foundation of the product and are not expected to.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Businesses" icon="fa-solid fa-store" href="/overview#the-partner-journey">
    The business is the subject of everything Float does: assessments, invoices and reputation all attach to one. Two partners who serve the same business can be linked through a wallet it controls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reputation" icon="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" href="/architecture#partner-reported-state-vs-float-verified-evidence">
    Portable, shared only with the business's consent, and built only from evidence Float verifies itself. Subscription and usage data a partner shares feed assessment only, never reputation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
