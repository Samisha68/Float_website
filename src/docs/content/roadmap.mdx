Float is pre-launch, and nothing is open for developer testing yet. This page shows where each part of the platform stands. Anything marked 🟡 is **coming soon**.

## Platform status

| Area | 🟢 Available now | 🟡 Coming soon |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Partner credentials issued directly by the Float team | Self-serve access and sign-up |
| **Businesses** | Registering and linking businesses, protected identifiers, wallet-based identity | n/a |
| **Credit assessments** | The full assessment process, including information requests and result handling | The production assessment engine and aggregate eligibility checks |
| **Risk bands** | Bands drawn from a versioned taxonomy, with expiry | The final band taxonomy |
| **Events & notifications** | An ordered event feed and signed notifications with automatic retries | Self-serve notification management |
| **Verifiable assessments** | Fingerprints, batching and proofs, with private detail kept by the owning partner | On-chain anchoring and revocation |
| **Documents** | Encrypted handling and forwarding | Secure document storage |
| **Invoices & repayments** | Recording invoices, tracking repayments and overdue status, collection, and Float's own on-chain verification | Automatic matching of invoice repayments seen on-chain |
| **Credit lines** | Credit ceilings, facilities, usage, live limits, settlements and on-chain observation | Faster on-chain indexing at scale |
| **Reputation** | Shared with consent, drawn only from Float-verified evidence | Reputation bands |

<Note>
On-chain observation is available but **switched on per environment**. Where it is off, repayments are marked as not verifiable and reputation stays empty. Today it is limited to Solana devnet.
</Note>

## Under review
