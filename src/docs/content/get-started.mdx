## What Float gives you

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Assessment" icon="fa-solid fa-file-invoice-dollar" href="/overview#two-layers-of-eligibility">
    Float detects which of a provider's customers are eligible for credit, and sets their limits automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Funds and repayment" icon="fa-solid fa-hand-holding-dollar" href="/overview#the-partner-journey">
    Float manages the whole credit line, providers just plug Float in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reputation" icon="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" href="/architecture#partner-reported-state-vs-float-verified-evidence">
    Portable reputation built and extended from repayments Float verifies.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Float's funding model will be published before launch.

## Explore by product

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Credit lines">
    Float gives a service provider's customers ongoing credit to use its API or RPC service, based on whether they are eligible.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Eligibility first" icon="fa-solid fa-user-check">
        Eligibility starts from past subscription records. Once a user accepts the terms, Float can reassess through service usage.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Automatic limits" icon="fa-solid fa-gauge">
        Limits are set automatically by Float's programmable credit logic, based on each customer's history. Providers can also set stricter limits.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Float extends the credit" icon="fa-solid fa-hand-holding-dollar">
        Float provides the funds. The customer uses the credit for the provider's service, and the provider is paid as normal.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Repay Float" icon="fa-solid fa-rotate">
        Customers repay through Float. Repayments are settled automatically through a Solana delegation the customer controls.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Invoice financing">
    With invoice financing, Float pays the provider's invoice, and the customer repays Float over time.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Connect once" icon="fa-solid fa-plug">
        The provider connects to Float once. Its business doesn't need to be created in Float first.
      </Card>
      <Card title="See who is eligible" icon="fa-solid fa-user-check">
        The provider uses its Float keys to see how many of its customers are likely eligible, and why.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Set stricter rules" icon="fa-solid fa-sliders">
        Providers can tighten Float's baseline and decide which plans can be financed. They can't loosen it.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Manage from a dashboard" icon="fa-solid fa-table-columns">
        Eligibility, rules and plans are managed in a dashboard.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Tip>Float funds the invoice and collects the repayments, so the provider takes no credit risk.</Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reputation">
    Reputation is for a service provider's **own customers**. It is a summary of their repayment outcomes, verified by Float on-chain. It never includes amounts, partners or invoices.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Consent first" icon="fa-solid fa-user-check">
        A customer's reputation can only be read with their consent.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Verified only" icon="fa-solid fa-circle-check">
        Subscription and usage data feed assessment only. Reputation comes only from repayments Float observes itself.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Float is pre-launch. Each part of the platform is at one of these stages.
