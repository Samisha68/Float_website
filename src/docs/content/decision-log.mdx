The handful of decisions most relevant if you're evaluating Float.

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Float manages assessment, funds and repayment.** Float assesses eligibility, extends the credit, pays the infrastructure provider for usage on credit and collects repayments. The provider takes no credit risk. | One party manages the whole credit lifecycle, so providers can offer credit without lending their own capital or chasing repayments. Float's funding model will be published before launch. |
| **Businesses repay Float on-chain in USDC.** Repayments settle through an SPL token delegation the business controls. | Settlement is automatic, the business keeps control and can see every pull, and Float can verify every repayment on-chain itself. |
| **Eligibility is checked in two layers.** Layer 1 uses subscription records in aggregate. Layer 2 reviews a business's individual usage, only after it opts in. | Providers can see likely eligibility from data they already keep, without sharing anything identifying. Detailed usage is reviewed only with the business's consent. |
| **Businesses opt in by connecting a wallet and signing a message.** | Consent is recorded in a form that is hard to spoof, and the business is linked to the wallet Float observes repayments from. |
| **Providers can tighten eligibility rules but not loosen them.** This includes choosing what counts as a qualifying subscription, such as whether renewals count. | Float's baseline protects every credit facility consistently, while providers can still be more selective with their own users. |
| **Every query is scoped to one provider.** | One provider can never see another's data. The only deliberate exception is reputation, shared across providers with the business's consent. |
| **Usage data is shared as daily aggregates only.** | Float needs patterns, not payloads. Request contents and end-user IP addresses are never shared. |
| **Providers choose how credit offers appear.** A Float-hosted page, embedded components styled to the provider's brand, or a custom build on the API. | Providers can launch quickly with Float's UI or keep full control of their own experience, while Float runs the credit itself in every case. |
| **Reputation uses Float-verified evidence only.** | Portable reputation has to be independently checkable, and that applies equally to good and bad outcomes. Subscription and usage data feed assessment only, never reputation. |
| **A wallet proof is the only link between providers.** | It is hard to spoof, and there are no merged business records to get wrong. |
| **Private and public data are kept apart.** Only fingerprints are ever public. | Providers and the businesses they serve stay in control of what is shared, and with whom. |
| **Credit lines and invoice financing share one platform.** They are two kinds of request on the same assessment and reputation system. | One set of businesses, consent rules, events and reputation, not two. |
| **On-chain observation is switched on per environment.** | Nothing silently depends on a network connection. |

## Superseded decisions

Earlier decisions kept for the record. They no longer describe how Float works.

| Decision | Why it was made | Superseded by |
|---|---|---|
| **Float is non-custodial.** It never disburses, holds or moves funds, and obligations are recorded only after a provider has financed. | Fewer moving parts, and no custody or lending risk sat with Float. | **Float manages assessment, funds and repayment.** |
